# Luma

Luma is my final project for CSE 340. I wanted to make a website I would actually use on a hard day. The idea is simple. You can take a moment to check in with yourself, find a gospel message that fits what you need, and write down one thing you’re grateful for.

## How it will work

When you sign in, two little glowing spots move around each other, and Luma welcomes you by your first name. You can write how you feel, pick more than one feeling, or skip that question.

Then Luma asks what would help right now. If you pick something like peace or hope, it can show you a scripture, a quote, or a General Conference talk. You can open the original source or save the message for later.

You can also keep a private gratitude journal. You’ll be able to look back at what you wrote and edit or delete your own entries.

## Who can do what

- **Members** can check in, save messages, write in their journal, and suggest a resource that helped them.
- **Contributors** can draft resources and review suggestions.
- **Admins** can manage accounts and roles, organize resources, and publish approved suggestions.

Members can see when their suggestion was submitted, when it was reviewed, and whether it was published or needs changes.

## What I’ll use

I’ll use Node.js and Express for the server, EJS for the pages, and PostgreSQL for the database. I plan to deploy Luma on Render.
